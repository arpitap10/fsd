The Land of Festivals

Nagaland: Ancient Tribes, Living Culture

Nagaland is India's most authentic cultural frontier. Sixteen distinct Naga tribes, each with their own language, costume, and warrior traditions, have preserved their heritage with fierce pride.

The Hornbill Festival in December transforms Kisama Village into the most colourful spectacle in the subcontinent. And then there is Dzukou — a secret valley of seasonal flowers that rewards only those willing to walk for it.

Getting There

Reaching Nagaland

Fly into Dimapur Airport from Kolkata, Delhi, or Guwahati. Then drive to Kohima (3 hrs). Inner Line Permit (ILP) required for all non-residents — get it in Delhi, Kolkata, or at Dimapur.
