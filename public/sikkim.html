Hidden Himalayan Kingdom

Sikkim: Sacred, Silent, Sublime

India's smallest state hides some of its most staggering landscapes. Gurudongmar Lake — one of the world's highest — reflects a sky so blue it seems painted. Kanchenjunga looms like a guardian god.

Ancient Rumtek and Pemayangtse monasteries echo with butter-lamp prayers. The Yumthang Valley explodes with rhododendrons every spring. Sikkim is sacred India at its most pure.

Getting There

Reaching Sikkim

Fly into Bagdogra Airport (IXB), then take a shared jeep or taxi to Gangtok (4 hrs). Pakyong Airport near Gangtok also has limited connectivity. Inner Line Permits required for North Sikkim.

Stay Safe, Travel Smart
🚨

Emergency Contacts

  • Sikkim Police: 100
  • Ambulance: 108
  • Gangtok HQ: 03592-201033
📋

Permit Tips

  • ILP required for North Sikkim, Nathula, Gurudongmar
  • Arrange permits through Gangtok travel agents
  • Carry extra passport photos and ID copies
❄️

Mountain Safety

  • Weather changes rapidly — carry rain gear
  • Altitude sickness above 4000m is real
  • Road closures in monsoon are common
