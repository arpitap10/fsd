Udaipur: A City Frozen in Time

Built around shimmering lakes and crowned by the Aravalli Hills, Udaipur is the most romantic city in India. The City Palace — a 400-year-old architectural marvel — commands Lake Pichola like a king.

Wander narrow bazaars selling miniature paintings, take a shikara across Jag Mandir, watch traditional Rajasthani folk performers under a full moon. Udaipur is pure theatre.

Getting There

Reaching Udaipur

Maharana Pratap Airport (UDR) connects to Delhi, Mumbai, and Jaipur. Well served by trains from Delhi (Chetak Express) and Mumbai (Mewar Express). Udaipur city is best explored by auto-rickshaw or cycle.

Scam Awareness

  • Fixed price autos exist — negotiate before boarding
  • Buy handicrafts from government emporiums
  • Be wary of 'carpet shop' tours

Weather Tips

  • Summers are extreme (45°C+) — travel Oct–Mar
  • Carry sunscreen and water at all times
  • Wear light cotton in warm months
