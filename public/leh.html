The Last Frontier

Ladakh: Where Earth Meets Sky

A high-altitude cold desert in the northernmost reaches of India, Leh-Ladakh is a landscape unlike anywhere else on Earth. Barren mountains shimmer in impossible colours at dawn.

Buddhist monasteries cling to cliffsides. Pangong Lake shifts from blue to turquoise to green. For solo travellers who want to be truly alone with the universe — this is it.

Getting There

Reaching Ladakh

Fly into Leh Kushok Bakula Rimpochee Airport (direct from Delhi, Mumbai, Srinagar). Road trips via Manali-Leh Highway or Srinagar-Leh Highway are legendary but require acclimatisation stops.

Stay Safe, Travel Smart
🚨

Emergency Contacts

  • Ladakh Police: 112
  • Ambulance: 108
  • Tourist Helpline: 1800-11-1363
⛰️

Altitude Safety

  • Spend 2 days in Leh before going higher
  • Carry altitude sickness tablets (Diamox)
  • Drink 3+ litres of water daily
🌡️

Weather Tips

  • Dress in layers — temperature swings wildly
  • UV protection essential at high altitude
  • Inform someone of your route always
