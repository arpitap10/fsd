Kerala: Where Nature Breathes

From the emerald backwaters of Alleppey to the mist-wrapped tea gardens of Munnar, Kerala is a land of extraordinary, quiet beauty. Every shade of green you can imagine.

Houseboat dreams on mirrored lagoons. Kathakali dancers with painted faces. Spice traders in Fort Kochi. Kerala is not just a destination — it's a feeling.

Getting There

Reaching Kerala

Kerala has 3 international airports: Thiruvananthapuram, Kochi (Cochin), and Calicut. Indian Railways covers the coast beautifully. The scenic train from Shoranur to Nilambur is unmissable.

Nature Tips

  • Leeches in forests — tuck trousers in socks
  • Monsoon (Jun-Sep) makes roads slippery
  • Wildlife sanctuaries need permits
🚢

Water Safety

  • Life jackets on all boat rides
  • Check houseboat safety ratings
  • Avoid swimming in backwaters alone
