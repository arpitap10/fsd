The Gateway to the Himalayas

Manali: Snow, Speed & Silence

Nestled in the Kullu Valley, Manali is Himachal Pradesh's crown jewel. Ringed by peaks draped in snow and pine forests that seem to touch the sky, it's a year-round playground.

From skiing in Solang Valley to the sacred Hadimba Temple's cedar forest, from roaring Beas River to hidden Naggar Castle — Manali has more layers than its mountains.

Getting There

Reaching Manali

Manali is best reached by overnight Volvo bus from Delhi (13-14 hrs). The nearest airport is Kullu-Manali Airport (Bhuntar), 50km away. Rohtang permit required for passes.

Local Flavours

What to Eat

Siddu

Himachali steamed bread stuffed with walnuts and poppy seeds — a mountain delicacy.

Trout Fish Curry

Fresh Himalayan trout from Beas River, lightly spiced and unforgettable.

Dham

Traditional Himachali feast served on leaf plates — rice, lentils, curd, and rajma.

Mountain Safety

  • Check weather before heading to Rohtang
  • Carry warm layers even in summer
  • Rohtang Pass requires advance permit

Solo Tips

  • Register at the police post before remote treks
  • Download offline maps
  • Carry basic first aid and medicines
